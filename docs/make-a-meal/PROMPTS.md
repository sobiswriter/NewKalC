## Review notes

The generated board is an artistic concept, so its small UI labels and ingredient numbers are not the source of truth. The interactive sample and product plan define quantities, readiness and time rules. The contact sheet is representative, and its dishes are not proof of exact recipe execution or allergen safety. The final app needs recipe validation, appropriate imagery and kitchen testing.
